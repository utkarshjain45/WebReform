# WebReform Algorithms & Optimization Engine

This package houses the graph restructuring algorithms used by **WebReform** to turn messy website navigation trees into clean, intuitive structures.

---

## What Does This Engine Do?

Imagine visiting an online store where the checkout page is buried 6 clicks deep inside an outdated blog post. Most users get frustrated and give up.

The **WebReform Engine** solves this automatically:
1. **Reads your website's layout**: Takes every page and internal link on your site.
2. **Evaluates navigation efficiency**: Calculates how many clicks it takes for visitors to reach high-value pages.
3. **Smart Reorganization**: Searches through thousands of possible menu arrangements to find the cleanest, most logical hierarchy.
4. **Protects user familiarity**: Makes sure related pages stay together and doesn't overwhelm visitors with menus that are too long.

---

## Core Optimization Objectives

When reorganizing a website, the engine balances four practical factors:

1. **Quick Access (Low Click Depth)**: Essential pages should always be reachable within 2 to 3 clicks from the homepage.
2. **Logical Grouping (Semantic Coherence)**: Pages about similar topics (e.g., pricing, billing, accounts) are kept together under relevant parent categories.
3. **Clean Menus (Cognitive Load Control)**: No menu has too many items at once, preventing visitors from feeling overwhelmed.
4. **Preserved Connections**: Important existing links and relationships are kept intact so loyal visitors aren't disoriented.
